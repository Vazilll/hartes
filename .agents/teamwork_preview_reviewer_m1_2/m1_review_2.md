### B. Clinical Dosing Safety & Biochemical Protocol Compliance
- **Zinc:Copper Ratio (10:1 to 15:1)**: Fully verified in Section 1.1F & Table 4.1. Explains enterocyte metallothionein (MT) induction by high zinc (>40-50 mg/day) sequestering copper, leading to microcytic/sideroblastic anemia and peripheral neuropathy. Standardized dose of 15–30 mg elemental Zinc paired with 1.5–2.0 mg elemental Copper.
- **Boron Cycling Protocol (2w ON / 1w OFF)**: Fully verified in Section 2.5C & Table 4.1. Cites Naghii et al. (2011) protocol showing 10 mg/day drops SHBG by 15–20% and raises free T by 28–30%. Mandatory 2w ON / 1w OFF cycling prevents homeostatic feedback resetting of SHBG and aromatase.
- **Ashwagandha Serotonergic Anhedonia Risk**: Fully verified in Section 3.1C & 3.1D. Details risk of 5-HT1A receptor desensitization and excessive diurnal cortisol flattening from chronic use (>8–12 weeks). Mandates cycling (6-8w ON / 2-4w OFF for KSM-66; 4-6w ON / 2w OFF for Sensoril).
- **Vitamin D3 + K2 MK-7 Synergy**: Fully verified in Section 2.2A. Explains VDR activation in Leydig cells upregulating 3β-HSD and 17β-HSD, while K2 MK-7 carboxylates Osteocalcin (stimulating Leydig GPRC6A receptors) and Matrix Gla Protein (MGP, preventing vascular calcification).
- **NMN/NR Methyl Donor Sparing with TMG**: Fully verified in Section 3.5A. Explains NNMT enzymatic clearance of nicotinamide consuming SAMe methyl groups. Mandates 1:1 to 1:2 co-administration of Trimethylglycine (TMG, 500–1,000 mg/day).
- **Purified vs. Raw Shilajit Toxicity**: Fully verified in Section 3.3C. Highlights severe heavy metal (Pb, As, Cd, Hg) and mycotoxin risks of raw shilajit, restricting approval exclusively to USP/EP standardized purified extracts (PrimaVie®, ≥50% fulvic acid, heavy metals <1 ppm).
